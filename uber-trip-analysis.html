02 — BUSINESS PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Start with the problem. Define the analytical purpose.

BUSINESS PROBLEM

What needs to be understood?

Trip activity needs to be explored across booking, value, distance, vehicle, location and time dimensions to understand demand patterns and trip characteristics.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

What this project sets out to deliver

Build an interactive Power BI report that organizes trip-level information into overview, time analysis and detailed trip views.

The project documentation and screenshots establish three report areas: overview, time analysis and trip details. 08 — BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Potential actions informed by the analysis.

These are suggested ways a stakeholder could use the analysis. They are not presented as actions already taken or measured project outcomes.

  1. Use the time-analysis view to identify periods that may require closer demand or capacity review.
  2. Compare vehicle and location patterns with booking volume and trip distance.
  3. Use the detailed trip view to validate aggregate patterns and investigate individual records.

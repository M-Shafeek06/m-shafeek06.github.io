Explore Uber trip data through a Power BI dashboard and present the analysis interactively.

Business / analytical focus

Explore trip bookings, booking value, distance, vehicle usage, locations and time-based demand through an interactive Power BI report.

What the project covers.

Time Analysis

Pickup-time patterns, day-of-week demand and hour-by-day analysis.

Trip Details

Detailed trip-level records including pickup date, pickup hour, vehicle, payment type, passengers, distance, booking value and location.

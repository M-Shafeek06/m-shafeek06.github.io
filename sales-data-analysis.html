07 — KEY FINDINGS & INSIGHTS

What the available project evidence supports.

The public project repository documents SQL analysis across sales, products, time and business dimensions and includes a reusable SQL script and PDF report. The portfolio source does not reproduce the underlying numeric outputs, so this page does not claim a top-performing product, region or month.

Evidence standard: this section distinguishes documented implementation and visible analytical scope from numeric conclusions. No unsupported KPI values, rankings or business outcomes are presented.

08 — BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Potential actions informed by the analysis.

These are suggested ways a stakeholder could use the analysis. They are not presented as actions already taken or measured project outcomes.

  1. Use product-level queries to identify which products warrant deeper performance review.
  2. Compare sales over time to investigate seasonality or unusual changes once the report outputs are validated.
  3. Review regional and category breakdowns together before proposing sales or inventory actions.

09 — LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE

The case-study documentation does not publish a verified dataset size, full schema or numerical query results. Recommendations are suggested analytical uses of the available query dimensions. Future scope could include a documented schema, query-performance notes, additional customer and profitability analysis, and a reproducible result summary.

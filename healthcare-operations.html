BUSINESS PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Why this project exists.

The business context and the intended outcome.

Business problem

Healthcare organizations generate information across operational, patient, clinical and financial functions. Reviewing these areas in isolation makes it harder to form a unified view of activity and performance.

Project objective

Create an integrated analytical reporting solution that brings multiple healthcare functions into one Power BI model and presents them through dedicated dashboard modules.

ANALYSIS & IMPLEMENTATION

What was actually built.

Implementation details and analytical components supported by the project artifacts.

Data preparation

Power Query-based cleaning and transformation prepare the healthcare data for analysis.

Data modeling

A 17-table model covers patient, hospital, physician, department, disease, treatment, admission, appointment, emergency, surgery, laboratory, pharmacy and billing entities.

Executive overview

Provides a consolidated view of overall healthcare performance.

Operational & patient modules

Hospital Operations and Patient Analytics cover hospital capacity, operational metrics, patient demographics and utilization.

Clinical service modules

Emergency, Surgery, Laboratory, Physician Performance and Pharmacy modules cover the respective service areas.

Financial performance

The financial module covers revenue, insurance, patient contribution and discounts. DAX measures support KPI comparisons, conditional formatting and time-based / year-over-year analysis.

09

LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE

What the project does not claim — and what could come next.

Clear boundaries make the work easier to evaluate and extend.

Current limitations

  • The repository README does not specify the underlying dataset source or record count.
  • The portfolio does not claim clinical effectiveness, patient outcome improvement or financial savings.
  • Dashboard measures support operational analysis and should not be interpreted as clinical guidance.

Potential future development

  • Extend the model with additional validated operational data where available.
  • Add documented data refresh and governance procedures.
  • Develop role-specific reporting views based on stakeholder requirements.
  • Document KPI definitions and data lineage in greater detail.

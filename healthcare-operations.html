Healthcare Operations Intelligence Platform

An enterprise-style healthcare analytics platform built in Power BI to provide a unified view of hospital operations, patient activity, clinical services, and financial performance.

Power BIPower QueryDAXExcelPower BI Service
View Repository ↗

01 — THE PROBLEM

Create a unified analytical view across multiple healthcare functions instead of treating operational, clinical, patient and financial reporting as isolated areas.

Business / analytical focus

Create a unified analytical view across multiple healthcare functions instead of treating operational, clinical, patient and financial reporting as isolated areas.

What the repository demonstrates

The repository documents a completed Power BI solution with a 17-table analytical data model, nine dashboard modules, KPI measures, time-based and year-over-year analysis, and Power BI Service/App publishing.

04 — SKILLS DEMONSTRATED

Evidence of practical analytics work.

Data preparation

Power Query-based cleaning and transformation.

Data modeling

17-table analytical model covering patients, hospitals, doctors, departments, diseases, treatments, admissions, appointments, emergency services, surgeries, laboratory services, pharmacy transactions and billing.

BI development

Power BI dashboards with interactive slicers and cross-filtering.

Business KPIs

DAX measures, KPI comparisons, conditional formatting and time-based analysis.

Deployment

Power BI Service deployment and a published Power BI App.

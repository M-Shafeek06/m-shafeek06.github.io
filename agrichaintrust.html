BUSINESS PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Why this project exists.

The business context and the intended outcome.

Business problem

Agricultural supply chains involve multiple hand-offs between participants. The project concept explores how a digital product experience can support traceability and product verification across that journey.

Project objective

Develop a supply-chain application concept with a frontend experience and separate backend services, organized around product verification and traceability.

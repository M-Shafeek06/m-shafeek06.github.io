LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE

What the project does not claim — and what could come next.

Clear boundaries make the work easier to evaluate and extend.

Current limitations

  • The public README does not specify dataset source, record count, date coverage or fields.
  • The current portfolio evidence does not include a verified numerical findings table.
  • Recommendations remain conditional until the PDF report and underlying workbook are reviewed in detail.

Potential future development

  • Publish a concise dataset dictionary and source citation.
  • Document the Excel preparation steps and formulas used.
  • Add a clear KPI and findings summary with values traceable to the report.
  • Consider an interactive dashboard if the underlying data and workbook support it.

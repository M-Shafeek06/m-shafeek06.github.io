08 — BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Potential actions informed by the analysis.

These are suggested ways a stakeholder could use the analysis. They are not presented as actions already taken or measured project outcomes.

  1. Use demographic filters to compare patterns while checking the underlying sample sizes.
  2. Review risk-indicator comparisons as descriptive analysis, not as medical diagnosis or causal evidence.
  3. Document metric definitions and dataset limitations before communicating findings to decision-makers.

BUSINESS PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Why this project exists.

The business context and the intended outcome.

Business problem

Health-related datasets can contain multiple dimensions that are difficult to compare in static tables. Interactive visual reporting allows users to explore survival measures and risk-related indicators across age groups and gender selections.

Project objective

Use Power BI to explore survival outcomes, age-group patterns, gender-based comparisons and selected health-related indicators through an interactive dashboard.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

How stakeholders could use the analysis.

These are potential actions informed by the project scope, not claims that a business outcome has already been achieved.

Keep comparisons descriptive

Interpret displayed patterns as dataset-level associations, not medical advice or causal evidence.

Review subgroup definitions

Confirm age bands, gender categories and indicator definitions before drawing comparisons.

Validate source context

Check data provenance and definitions before using the dashboard for any substantive healthcare decision.

09

LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE

What the project does not claim — and what could come next.

Clear boundaries make the work easier to evaluate and extend.

Current limitations

  • Dataset source, sample size and clinical definitions are not documented in the public README.
  • The dashboard is descriptive and should not be used for diagnosis, treatment or individual risk prediction.
  • No causal relationship or clinical effectiveness claim is made.

Potential future development

  • Document dataset provenance, sample size and field definitions.
  • Add a transparent metric dictionary for survival and risk indicators.
  • Include statistical context and caveats for subgroup comparisons.
  • Validate any future predictive extension with appropriate domain expertise and evaluation.

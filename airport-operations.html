FEATURED CASE STUDY · 01

Airport Operations Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end Business Intelligence solution that transforms historical flight operational data into structured, decision-ready reporting through Python ETL, MySQL, dimensional modeling and Power BI.

PythonPandasSQLMySQLPower QueryDAXPower BIAzure Maps
View Repository ↗

01 — THE PROBLEM

Turn raw flight data into operational visibility.

Business focus

The platform is designed to help stakeholders monitor flight operations, airline performance, airport performance, delays, operational efficiency and executive KPIs.

Analytics outcome

Instead of working directly from raw datasets, the project centralizes transformed operational data into a Power BI reporting solution for structured analysis and decision support.

From raw data to executive reporting.

03 — WHAT I BUILT

Four analytical dashboard areas.

Executive Overview

Executive KPIs including total flights, on-time percentage, delay percentage, cancellation rate, total delay minutes, average delay, total distance and average air time.

Delay Analysis

Delay trends, delay categories, worst routes, delay hotspots, airport rankings and airline rankings.

Airline Performance

On-time performance, delay rate, cancellation rate, operational score, airline efficiency matrix and top/bottom airline comparisons.

Airport Performance

Airport operational score, traffic volume, on-time performance, delay rate, airport rankings and geographic visualization with Azure Maps.

05 — ANALYTICAL SKILLS DEMONSTRATED

A portfolio project that shows the full BI workflow.

Data preparation

Python/Pandas ETL scripts are used for transformation and validation, with separate loading and fact-table transformation workflows in the repository.

Data engineering

MySQL is used for relational storage and SQL for querying, followed by a star-schema analytical model.

BI development

Power BI, Power Query and DAX are used to build interactive reporting and dynamic business KPIs.

Business reporting

The project focuses the analysis on operational monitoring, benchmarking, bottleneck identification and executive reporting.

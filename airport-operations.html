BUSINESS PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Why this project exists.

The business context and the intended outcome.

Business problem

Flight operations involve multiple performance dimensions: traffic volume, on-time performance, delays, cancellations, airline efficiency and airport-level performance. Raw operational records are difficult to monitor consistently without a prepared analytical model and structured reporting.

Project objective

Build an end-to-end BI workflow that prepares historical flight data and organizes it into a centralized Power BI reporting solution for operational monitoring, comparison and executive review.

ANALYSIS & IMPLEMENTATION

What was actually built.

Implementation details and analytical components supported by the project artifacts.

Python ETL

Repository scripts support transformation, fact-table preparation, validation and loading into MySQL.

Relational storage

MySQL provides the relational database layer used by the reporting workflow.

Dimensional modeling

A star-schema model organizes prepared flight facts and related dimensions for analytical reporting.

Executive overview

Reports total flights, on-time percentage, delay percentage, cancellation rate, total delay minutes, average delay, total distance and average air time.

Delay analysis

Explores delay trends, delay categories, worst routes, delay hotspots and airport/airline rankings.

Airline & airport performance

Compares on-time performance, delay and cancellation rates, operational scores, traffic volume and airport rankings; Azure Maps supports geographic visualization.

09

LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE

What the project does not claim — and what could come next.

Clear boundaries make the work easier to evaluate and extend.

Current limitations

  • The published project description does not specify the exact dataset row count or complete source provenance.
  • The dashboards describe historical performance and do not provide real-time flight tracking.
  • No numerical business outcome or quantified operational improvement is claimed.

Potential future development

  • Integrate real-time flight tracking.
  • Explore predictive delay analysis and weather data integration.
  • Automate ETL scheduling.
  • Consider a mobile dashboard layout and machine-learning extensions.
  • Evaluate Power BI Service deployment for broader access.

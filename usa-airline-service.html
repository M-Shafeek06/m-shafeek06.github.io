02 — ANALYTICAL CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE

Understand how flight activity and service disruptions vary across airlines, airports and time.

Airline service performance is reflected not only in the number of flights operated, but also in whether departures are on time and how frequently delays or cancellations occur.

THE ANALYTICAL CHALLENGE

Look beyond total flight counts.

A flight-volume view alone does not explain service reliability. Delays, cancellations, average delay time and the distribution of flights across airports provide additional dimensions for examining operational performance.

THE OBJECTIVE

Build an interactive view of airline service.

The project uses Power BI to analyze US airline performance from 2009–2011, bringing together total flights, on-time departures, delays, cancellations, average delay times, airport-wise flight distribution and time-based patterns.

03 — REPORT & DATA STRUCTURE

A focused Power BI reporting solution for historical airline service analysis.

The public project repository provides the Power BI report file, its dashboard image and a project README. The documented deliverable is an interactive report rather than a separately documented multi-system data platform.

Analysis scope

Historical US airline performance covering the 2009–2011 period, as described in the project repository.

Reporting layer

Power BI is used to present the airline service analysis in an interactive dashboard.

Performance measures

The documented dashboard focuses on total flights, on-time departures, delays, cancellations and average delay times.

Analytical dimensions

Airport-wise flight distribution, trends over time and day-of-week analysis provide different ways to examine service patterns.

Implementation note: the repository does not separately document a source-to-report ETL architecture, database layer or data-model diagram. This case study therefore describes only the Power BI reporting solution and analysis confirmed by the available project materials.

04 — ANALYTICAL & REPORT DESIGN

Organize the analysis around the main dimensions of airline service performance.

The dashboard brings together operational volume and service outcomes, while time and airport views help examine where patterns differ.

Flight activity

Use total flight volume to establish the scale of activity represented in the analysis.

Departure reliability

Review on-time departures alongside delays to understand service performance from a departure perspective.

Disruption monitoring

Include cancellations and average delay times as additional indicators of disruption and delay severity.

Time and airport views

Explore changes over time, compare days of the week and examine how flights are distributed across airports.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Read flight volume together with service outcomes, then use time-based and airport-level views to add operational context.

Documented measures

The stated scope includes total flights, on-time departures, delays, cancellations, average delay times and airport-wise flight distribution.

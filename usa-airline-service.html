USA Airline Service Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing US airline performance from 2009–2011, with emphasis on service reliability, delays, cancellations and flight distribution.

Power BIAirline AnalyticsTime AnalysisService Reliability

01 — THE PROBLEM

Analyze airline service reliability across flight volume, on-time departures, delays, cancellations, average delay time and airport distribution.

Business / analytical focus

Evaluate service reliability by combining flight volume, on-time departures, delays, cancellations, average delay times and airport distribution.

What the repository demonstrates

The public repository describes an interactive dashboard for 2009–2011 with trend analysis and day-of-week analysis to reveal patterns in service reliability.

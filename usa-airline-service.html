08 — BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Potential actions informed by the analysis.

These are suggested ways a stakeholder could use the analysis. They are not presented as actions already taken or measured project outcomes.

  1. Use the time and day-of-week views to identify periods that merit closer operational review.
  2. Compare airport-level flight distribution with reliability measures to understand where further analysis may be useful.
  3. Review delays and cancellations alongside total flight volume rather than interpreting a single metric in isolation.

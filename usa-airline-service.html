BUSINESS PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Why this project exists.

The business context and the intended outcome.

Business problem

Airline service performance is shaped by flight volume, punctuality, cancellations and delay patterns. Looking at only one measure can hide differences across airports and time periods.

Project objective

Use an interactive Power BI report to explore US airline service reliability over the 2009–2011 period through operational measures, airport distribution and time-based analysis.

07

KEY FINDINGS & INSIGHTS

The service-performance dimensions exposed by the dashboard

The public repository confirms the dashboard scope and measures, but does not document specific numerical conclusions. The portfolio therefore focuses on the comparisons the report supports, not invented airline rankings or delay statistics.

Volume context

Total flights and airport-wise distribution provide the traffic context.

Punctuality

On-time departure measures provide a service-reliability view.

Disruption

Delays and cancellations provide complementary indicators of service disruption.

Time patterns

Trend and day-of-week views help examine how measures vary over time.

09

LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE

What the project does not claim — and what could come next.

Clear boundaries make the work easier to evaluate and extend.

Current limitations

  • The published README does not identify the dataset source or row count.
  • The dashboard covers historical data from 2009–2011 and should not be presented as a current airline performance report.
  • No quantified operational improvement or specific carrier ranking is claimed.

Potential future development

  • Add a clearly documented dataset source and field dictionary.
  • Extend the analysis with additional validated operational dimensions if available.
  • Add refreshed data to support more recent comparisons.
  • Document KPI definitions and calculation logic.
